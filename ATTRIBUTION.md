# Attribution

Planet Zero's feed holds data from these sources, each under its own terms, checked on 2026-10-15. Every JSON file also carries a `credits` block for the sources it holds, with the date the data was fetched. Nobody downloading these files gets more rights than each source grants: see each licence.

## NEA (Singapore)

Contains information from 1-hour PM2.5 readings and Pollutant Standards Index (PSI) accessed on the date given in each file from data.gov.sg which is made available under the terms of the Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0 (https://data.gov.sg/open-data-licence).

Licence: Singapore Open Data Licence version 1.0. Live display and a public archive allowed with this notice. No NEA endorsement is implied, and no rights pass to people who download our files: see the licence.

## DOE (Malaysia)

Air Pollutant Index (API) from the Department of Environment Malaysia (DOE), via APIMS (https://eqms.doe.gov.my/APIMS/main). Data dan maklumat tertakluk di bawah Terma Penggunaan Data Terbuka Kerajaan Malaysia 1.0 (https://www.doe.gov.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/TermaPenggunaan.pdf).

Licence: Malaysian Government Open Data Terms of Use 1.0 (Terma Penggunaan Data Terbuka Kerajaan Malaysia 1.0). DOE lists APIMS as its open dataset under these terms, which allow copying and publishing with their credit line. The portal itself states no terms, and the address we read is not documented.

## OpenAQ (community sensors)

Community sensor data via OpenAQ (https://openaq.org), licence per location: AirGradient locations under CC BY 4.0 (data provided by AirGradient via OpenAQ, https://www.airgradient.com), Clarity locations under CC0 1.0. Each sensor's owner is named with its readings.

Licence: Per location: CC BY 4.0 (AirGradient) or CC0 1.0 (Clarity). Shown with credit to OpenAQ and the sensor's owner. Only locations under CC BY 4.0 or CC0 1.0 are kept in our history: none without a licence (HabitatMap) and none share-alike (Sensor.Community).

## CAMS via Open-Meteo (computer model)

Licence: CC BY 4.0 (Open-Meteo); Licence to Use Copernicus Products. Open-Meteo's free service, for non-commercial use: this site has no ads, no paid tier and no accounts. Labelled a computer model's estimate, never a measurement.

Licence: CC BY 4.0. Open-Meteo's free service, for non-commercial use. Labelled a computer model's estimate.

## NASA FIRMS (fires)

We acknowledge the use of data and/or imagery from NASA's Fire Information for Resource Management System (FIRMS) (https://earthdata.nasa.gov/firms), part of NASA's Land, Atmosphere Near real-time Capability for Earth observations (LANCE) (https://earthdata.nasa.gov/lance) and NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS). Datasets: S-NPP VIIRS NRT, doi:10.5067/FIRMS/VIIRS/VNP14IMGT_NRT.002; NOAA-20 VIIRS NRT, doi:10.5067/FIRMS/VIIRS/VJ114IMGT_NRT.002. Disclaimer: https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/data/projects/lance/disclaimer. Near real-time detections; NASA's later standard products may differ.

Licence: NASA open data, with FIRMS's acknowledgement. Open data. No NASA endorsement is implied; not for tactical decisions.

## aqicn (World Air Quality Index project)

Current values from the World Air Quality Index project (https://aqicn.org), relaying NEA and DOE. Data not verified by aqicn; not for paid use; its archive is not kept.

Licence: aqicn's terms: credit aqicn and the agency; no paid use; no copying of its archive. The current value only, with credit to aqicn and to NEA or DOE; never kept in our history.

## Ours

Our checks of numbers we may not show (Google's Air Quality API) against NEA's monitors and community sensors. Only our results are published, never Google's values.

Advice: NEA's hourly guide, in plain words (www.haze.gov.sg); DOE's advice quoted from its "General Information of Air Pollutant Index" (doe.gov.my).
